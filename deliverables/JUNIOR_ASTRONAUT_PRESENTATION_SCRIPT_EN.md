# Junior Astronaut — Presentation Script

Allow about 3½ minutes for speaking and 14 seconds for the gameplay clip.

## 1. First Night on Mars — 20 seconds

“Imagine landing on Mars with four astronauts just before sunset. Will the base have enough electricity to last until morning? In Junior Astronaut, children make that decision and see what happens on the map.”

## 2. The Mars Base Map — 25 seconds

*Point to the habitat, the crew and the mission panel.*

“Hover over a map icon to see its name, or tap it on a phone. The mission panel gives you one task at a time. Your first job is to power the habitat.”

## 3. Building the Base — 30 seconds

*Point to the sidebar and the crew at the building site.*

“The mission asks for solar panels. Choose the kit, then choose one of the marked sites. Two astronauts carry the parts and assemble the panels. Once they finish, you connect the panels to the habitat.”

## 4. Power Through the Night — 35 seconds

“What happens when the Sun sets? The panels stop producing power, so the battery has to carry the base through the night. Charging Scout uses two units of energy. You can top up the battery before dark or wait until morning to charge Scout. During the storm scenario, you also decide when to shelter the crew.”

## 5. Research Before Building — 35 seconds

*Point to Scout and the research progress bar.*

“At sunrise, Scout surveys a possible building site. You send astronauts to check the water supply and test a grow light. Once you have all three findings, the greenhouse kit becomes available. NASA resources informed these lessons. Scout’s readings are simulated, and the science panel retrieves study metadata from NASA’s Open Science Data Repository.”

[Mars Trek](https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/jpl/config/textAbout.html) · [Growing Plants in Space](https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/growing-plants-in-space/) · [OSD-314](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-314)

## 6. A Greenhouse and the First Crop — 35 seconds

“Choose where to build the greenhouse and watch the crew prepare its foundation, assemble the frame and finish the dome. When the greenhouse opens, water the plants and turn on the grow light. After the harvest, the final question takes us back to the first challenge. What kept the base powered at night?”

## 7. Gameplay Demo — 25 seconds plus the clip

*Play approximately 1:50–2:04 of the [gameplay recording](./Junior-Astronaut-gameplay.webm).* 

“Here, the player chooses the greenhouse site and the crew carries out the work. You can follow each stage as the building takes shape. What would you check before trying to grow plants on Mars?”
